## Why This Exists

Generative AI is evolving at breakneck speed. New tools, agents, coding assistants, image generators, and multimodal systems appear every week. Navigating this landscape can be overwhelming, especially for students, engineers transitioning into AI, or enthusiasts who want practical, no-nonsense starting points.

These free AI ebooks distill key platforms and workflows into accessible formats. Whether you are preparing for an AI course, building side projects, learning prompt engineering, or simply want to understand what Claude, Grok, Gemini, Cursor, Replit, and other AI tools can actually do in 2026, this open-source collection is for you.

## Origin Story

I attended an offline workshop on AI tools at the Indian Institute of Technology Ropar (IIT Ropar). Over three intensive days, the sessions covered a wide range of modern Generative AI platforms, coding agents, creative tools, and practical workflows.

To solidify my own learning and to make the material useful for others, I systematically documented everything into a series of beginner-friendly ebooks. Each guide focuses on a specific tool or ecosystem in an accessible "For Dummies" style. What began as personal notes from the IIT Ropar AI workshop has grown into this open-source collection of Generative AI foundations, freely available for AI enthusiasts, students, and builders worldwide.
